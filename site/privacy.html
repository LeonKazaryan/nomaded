Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

The short version: Nomaded collects no personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Everything you add stays on your iPhone.

Nomaded is a travel app for iPhone made by Leon Kazaryan, an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What the app keeps on your iPhone

  • the countries you mark as visited, and when you marked them;
  • the cities you add;
  • the medals you earn;
  • a few settings, such as whether you’ve finished the introduction and your App Store region (see below).

This is stored only in the app’s own storage on your iPhone. There is no server, and I never receive any of it. Like any app data, it can be part of your device backups (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer) if you have them turned on. Apple handles those backups under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

City search

When you search for a city, the app first looks in its own built-in list. To find other places, the text you type is sent to Apple Maps, which suggests matching places. That request goes to Apple, not to me, and is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy. Nomaded doesn’t keep your searches or send them anywhere else.

App Store region

Some borders are disputed, and in some countries the law says how maps must show them. To follow these laws, Nomaded reads the country or region of the App Store on your iPhone and draws the borders accordingly. This happens on your iPhone. The region is remembered there, so the right borders show even offline, and it is never sent anywhere.

What the app doesn’t do

  • It doesn’t ask for your location, photos, contacts or any other permission.
  • It has no third-party code: no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • It doesn’t share or sell data, because it doesn’t collect any.

Deleting your data

You can unmark countries and remove cities in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

Nomaded is suitable for all ages and doesn’t collect data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
