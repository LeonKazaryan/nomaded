Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

The short version: Nomaded collects no personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Everything you add stays on your iPhone or iPad.

Nomaded is a travel app for iPhone and iPad made by Leon Kazaryan, an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What the app keeps on your device

  • the countries you mark as visited, and when you marked them;
  • the cities you add;
  • the medals you earn;
  • a few settings, such as whether you’ve finished the introduction and your App Store region (see below).

This is stored in the app’s own storage on your iPhone or iPad. Starting with version 1.2, if iCloud is turned on for Nomaded, the countries, cities and medals are also kept in your private iCloud database, so your other devices show the same world and it comes back if you reinstall the app. That database belongs to your Apple Account: only you can see it, and I have no access to it. There is no server of mine, and I never receive any of it. Like any app data, it can also be part of your device backups (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer) if you have them turned on. Apple handles iCloud and backups under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

City search

When you search for a city, the app first looks in its own built-in list. To find other places, the text you type is sent to Apple Maps, which suggests matching places. That request goes to Apple, not to me, and is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy. Nomaded doesn’t keep your searches or send them anywhere else.

Sharing a story

Starting with version 1.2, you can share your globe as a story. The video and the picture are made on your device and go only where you send them. If you choose Save in the share sheet, iOS asks once to let Nomaded add them to your Photos. That permission only adds: the app can’t see your photos.

App Store region

Some borders are disputed, and in some countries the law says how maps must show them. To follow these laws, Nomaded reads the country or region of the App Store on your device and draws the borders accordingly. This happens on your device. The region is remembered there, so the right borders show even offline, and it is never sent anywhere.

What the app doesn’t do

  • It doesn’t ask for your location, contacts or any other permission, and never reads your photos. The only request is to add a story to Photos, when you choose to save one.
  • It has no third-party code: no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • It doesn’t share or sell data, because it doesn’t collect any.

Deleting your data

You can unmark countries and remove cities in the app at any time; with iCloud on, that happens on all your devices. Deleting the app removes its data from that device, but your copy in iCloud stays, so the app can bring it back. To delete that copy too, open Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Nomaded and delete its data. You can also turn iCloud off for Nomaded there.

Children

Nomaded is suitable for all ages and doesn’t collect data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
